JavaScript Full Stack AI Engineer — VentureDive

Job Brief
We're seeking a JavaScript Full Stack AI Engineer to lead the development of AI-powered applications and integrations. You'll work at the intersection of modern web development and cutting-edge LLM technologies, building production-grade systems that leverage the latest advances in generative AI. Your role spans from architecting backend services that integrate with LLMs to creating intuitive frontend experiences powered by AI.
This is a hands-on technical role for someone who thrives on understanding how LLMs work under the hood and translating that knowledge into practical solutions. You'll be expected to stay current with AI developments, experiment with new models and techniques, and guide architectural decisions around AI integration.

VentureDive Overview
Founded in 2012 by veteran technology entrepreneurs from MIT and Stanford, VentureDive is the fastest-growing technology company in the region that develops and invests in products and solutions that simplify and improve the lives of people worldwide. We aspire to create a technology organization and an entrepreneurial ecosystem in the region that is recognized as second to none in the world.

Key Responsibilities
- Design and implement full-stack applications that integrate large language models into production systems
- Build backend services (Node.js, Express, Python) that interface with LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, local models) and handle complex prompt engineering
- Develop frontend applications (React, Next.js) that provide real-time, interactive experiences with AI capabilities
- Run and fine-tune open-source LLMs locally using frameworks like Ollama, LM Studio, or vLLM for cost optimization and privacy
- Implement AI-assisted coding workflows using GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or similar tools to accelerate development
- Architect solutions for prompt optimization, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), fine-tuning, and model selection
- Ensure scalability, security, and cost-efficiency of AI integrations
- Collaborate with product, design, and ML teams to translate business requirements into AI-powered features
